Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-24

This Privacy Policy explains how Roamly: Street View Map (“Roamly” or “the app”), published by devJ666, handles information when you use the app on iPhone and iPad. In short: Roamly has no user accounts, runs no servers of its own, and does not collect personal data for us. Map features rely on Google and Apple services described below.

1. Location Information

With your permission, the app accesses your device location only while the app is in use to center the map, display nearby Street View panoramas and Apple Look Around imagery, find nearby places, and calculate routes. Your location is never sent to our servers.

2. Third-Party Map Providers

To render maps, panoramas, place searches, and geocoding, coordinates and map queries are sent directly from your device to Google Maps (Google Maps SDK and Street View) and Apple (MapKit, Look Around, and geocoding). Those requests are governed by each provider's privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

3. Data Collected by the Google Maps SDK

The app includes the Google Maps SDK for iOS to display Google maps and Street View panoramas. According to Google's SDK disclosure, the SDK may collect the following data, which Google processes under its own privacy policy:

  • A device identifier, used for analytics and app functionality
  • Crash data and performance data, used for analytics
  • Product interaction and other usage data, used for analytics

We do not receive or store this data and do not use it for tracking or advertising.

4. On-Device Storage

Your search history, saved places, and app preferences (such as selected map provider and language) are stored exclusively on your device in a local database and standard iOS storage. They are not uploaded to us or to any third party.

5. How We Use and Share Information

Information is used only to provide the features you request: showing your position, panoramas, nearby places, routes, saved places, and preferences. We do not sell, rent, or share personal information for advertising or marketing. Data reaches Google and Apple only as needed to deliver map features.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored on your device remains there until you delete it. You can remove saved places and clear recent searches inside the app, or delete all app data by uninstalling Roamly. We keep no copies on our own servers. Data processed by Google and Apple is retained according to their policies. If you email us, we keep your message only as long as needed to respond.

7. Your Choices and Rights

Location access is optional. You can allow it, limit it to approximate location, or turn it off at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Roamly. The app still works when you search for a place or choose a city.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we do not hold personal data about you, requests about data processed by Google or Apple should be sent to them; we are happy to help if you contact us.

8. No Ads, Tracking, or Accounts

Roamly does not require user accounts, does not display third-party advertisements, does not access your advertising identifier, and does not track your activity across other companies' apps or websites.

9. Security

Requests to map providers use encrypted HTTPS connections, and data stored on your device is protected by iOS security features.

10. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 years of age (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction) and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be published on this page and included in app releases with a revised effective date.

12. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or a privacy request? Email devj666@gmail.com.
