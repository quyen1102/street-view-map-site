Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-21

1. Location Information

With your permission, the app accesses your device location only while the app is in use to center the map, display nearby Street View panoramas and Apple Look Around imagery, find nearby places, and calculate routes. Your location is never sent to our servers.

2. Third-Party Map Providers

To render maps, panoramas, place searches, and geocoding, coordinates and map queries are sent directly from your device to Google Maps (Google Maps SDK and Street View) and Apple (MapKit, Look Around, and geocoding). Those requests are governed by each provider's privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

3. On-Device Storage

Your search history, saved places, and app preferences (such as selected map provider and language) are stored exclusively on your device in a local database and standard iOS storage. You can delete saved places or search history inside the app at any time, or remove all stored data by uninstalling the app.

4. No Ads, Tracking, or Accounts

Street View Map does not require user accounts, does not display third-party advertisements, and does not track your activity across other apps or websites.

5. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

7. Contact
