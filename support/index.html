Help for Roamly on iPhone and iPad.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't the app find my location?

Open iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Roamly and choose While Using the App. Make sure Location Services is on.

Why is Street View unavailable at some places?

Imagery comes from Google Street View and Apple Look Around, which do not cover every location. Try the other map provider from the Home screen, or move the pin to a nearby road.

How do I change the language?

Open the menu on the Home screen and choose Language. The app switches immediately.

Where is my saved data stored?

Saved places and search history stay on your device only. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I rely on routes for navigation?

Routes and the compass are for general guidance only. Always follow local traffic laws and real-world conditions.
